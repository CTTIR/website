## Clinical Data Science Course

[Clinical Data Science in R · Course](https://cttir.github.io/clinical-data-science-in-r-course/) — eight workshops on clinical AI and translational trauma research (diagnostic accuracy, grouped validation, longitudinal and mechanistic models, simulation, regulation, data protection and incident response), each with an R lab on synthetic data. It follows the four-stage progression of the biostatistics curriculum above.

Source: [CTTIR/clinical-data-science-in-r-course](https://github.com/CTTIR/clinical-data-science-in-r-course). Course text, tables and figures are licensed under CC BY 4.0; code is MIT-licensed.

## Game Theory Course

[#equilibria](https://cttir.github.io/equilibria/) — tutorials on game theory, decision-making, simulation, AI/ML and the data behind them, with runnable R examples, arranged in four courses of eight topic areas each.

<table class="cttir-toc">
<tbody>
<tr><td><a href="https://cttir.github.io/equilibria/courses/course1-game-theory/">Course 1</a></td><td>— Foundations of Game Theory: equilibrium concepts and the canonical games, decisions under uncertainty, evolutionary, cooperative, behavioural and network games, and the history behind them.</td></tr>
<tr><td><a href="https://cttir.github.io/equilibria/courses/course2-mechanisms-behaviour/">Course 2</a></td><td>— Mechanisms, Behaviour &amp; Ethics: designing the rules of the game, auctions, behaviour in the lab and the field, incentives in blockchains, ethics, and cases where theory meets reality.</td></tr>
<tr><td><a href="https://cttir.github.io/equilibria/courses/course3-methods/">Course 3</a></td><td>— Mathematical &amp; Statistical Methods: linear algebra, optimisation, information theory, statistics, Bayesian and causal inference, econometrics and network science.</td></tr>
<tr><td><a href="https://cttir.github.io/equilibria/courses/course4-simulation-ai-data/">Course 4</a></td><td>— Simulation, AI &amp; Data Practice: Monte Carlo and agent-based simulation, machine learning and multi-agent AI, public data, visualisation, reproducible workflows and R packages.</td></tr>
