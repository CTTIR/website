## Clinical Data Science Course

[Clinical Data Science in R · Course](https://cttir.github.io/clinical-data-science-in-r-course/) — eight workshops on clinical AI and translational trauma research (diagnostic accuracy, grouped validation, longitudinal and mechanistic models, simulation, regulation, data protection and incident response), each with an R lab on synthetic data. It follows the four-stage progression of the biostatistics curriculum above.

Source: [CTTIR/clinical-data-science-in-r-course](https://github.com/CTTIR/clinical-data-science-in-r-course). Course text, tables and figures are licensed under CC BY 4.0; code is MIT-licensed.
