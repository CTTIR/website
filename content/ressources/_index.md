The [CTTIR resource hub](https://cttir.github.io/ressources/) brings together
curated data sources, methods, software and reading for computational biomedical
research. This page is a guide to its maintained collections; the entries and
bibliography live in [CTTIR/ressources](https://github.com/CTTIR/ressources).

## Datasets and data sources

- [Public data sources](https://cttir.github.io/ressources/ressources/data-sources.html)
  — starting points for discovering reusable biomedical research data.
- [Omics and bioinformatics](https://cttir.github.io/ressources/ressources/omics-and-bioinformatics.html)
  — resources for molecular and cellular data workflows.

These are links to source collections, rather than a local repository of study
data. Check each source's access conditions, licence and documentation before use.

## Methods and reproducible workflows

- [Statistics and machine learning](https://cttir.github.io/ressources/ressources/stats-and-ml.html)
  — statistical modelling and computational method resources.
- [Reproducibility](https://cttir.github.io/ressources/ressources/reproducibility.html)
  — tools and practices for transparent, repeatable analyses.
- [cttiR workflow orchestration](https://github.com/CTTIR/cttiR)
  — project structure, reviewed analysis stages and versioned package knowledge
  connecting the CTTIR suite.

## Reading and references

- [Topical bibliography](https://cttir.github.io/ressources/references/)
  — reading organised around dimensionality reduction, omics, single-cell
  analysis, molecular pathology, R development, reproducibility and tissue injury.
- [Writing and publishing](https://cttir.github.io/ressources/ressources/writing-and-publishing.html)
  — resources for preparing and communicating research.
- [Download the bibliography (BibTeX)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CTTIR/ressources/main/references.bib)
  — reusable citation records from the resource hub.
