Open books maintained by CTTIR, with reproducible examples in R. See each book's repository for its content and code licences.

## Methods in R

A bench reference for biomedical authors and peer reviewers — every common method covered with R code, diagnostics, and reviewer-facing red flags.

[Read Methods in R](https://cttir.github.io/methods-in-r/) · [Source repository](https://github.com/CTTIR/methods-in-r)

## Clinical Data Science in R

Methods in clinical AI and translational trauma research — 28 chapters from the clinical question through model evaluation, imaging and simulation to ethics, regulation, data protection and security, each with an R lab on synthetic data. Companion course: [Clinical Data Science in R · Course](https://cttir.github.io/clinical-data-science-in-r-course/).

[Read Clinical Data Science in R](https://cttir.github.io/clinical-data-science-in-r/) · [Source repository](https://github.com/CTTIR/clinical-data-science-in-r)

## Scientometrics in R

A hands-on guide to scientometrics in R: data acquisition, bibliometric indicators, network analysis, text mining, and reproducible reporting.

[Read Scientometrics in R](https://cttir.github.io/scientometrics-in-r/) · [Source repository](https://github.com/CTTIR/scientometrics-in-r)

## Strategy in R

An open-source book on game theory, simulation, and machine intelligence — implemented in R.

[Read Strategy in R](https://cttir.github.io/strategy-in-r/) · [Source repository](https://github.com/CTTIR/strategy-in-r)
