Tutorials on R in general, written for anyone who works with data in R and not tied to CTTIR projects. They form a knowledge base and starting point that also serves as an in-depth reference: more than 550 runnable method pages in 16 topic areas, a test-selection decision tree and companion Shiny apps. Source: [CTTIR/tutorials](https://github.com/CTTIR/tutorials). Content and code licences are stated alongside each resource and in its repository.

## R Tutorials

A topic-organised library of standalone tutorials in R, from foundations and visualisation to modelling, machine learning and bioinformatics. Site: <https://cttir.github.io/tutorials/>.
